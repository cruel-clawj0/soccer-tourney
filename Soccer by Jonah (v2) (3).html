Your super meter fills over time, and faster when you concede. When it is full, press your super key while you have the ball. Every team has its own super move. In 1 player mode the arrow keys also move Player 1. A tackle knocks the ball off a close opponent. A special formation lasts 12 seconds, then needs 25 seconds to recharge. Press Esc during a match to pause. Press M to mute or unmute sound. Throw-ins, corners, free kicks and penalties: turn with your move keys, then press shoot for a long ball or pass to find a teammate. Too many tackles lead to fouls, cards and penalties. Penalty kicks: aim with A and D or the arrow keys, press Space to start the power bar, and Space again to shoot.

Team stats

Speed changes how fast a team runs, attack changes shot power and accuracy, and defense changes how often tackles win the ball.
